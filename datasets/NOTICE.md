# Sample dataset provenance and license

`GIS-cup-sample-dataset.geojson` was synthesized from a subset of Microsoft's [Global ML Building Footprints](https://github.com/microsoft/GlobalMLBuildingFootprints) dataset. The sample is a transformed derivative and is not an unchanged copy of the upstream data.

Microsoft makes the source data available under the [Community Data License Agreement - Permissive, Version 2.0](CDLA-Permissive-2.0.txt). That agreement applies to the Microsoft-derived sample dataset and permits sharing data with or without modifications when the agreement text is made available with the shared data.
